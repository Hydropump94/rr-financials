RR Financials

Internal back office software for Ruoff Restaurants

Connecting and reconnecting

RR Financials is connected to QuickBooks Online by Ruoff Restaurants staff from a company computer. There is no connect button on this page, and no one outside the company can start a connection.

How a connection is made

An authorized person runs the software's authorization step on a company machine. That step sends them to Intuit's own consent screen, where they sign in to QuickBooks and choose the company to connect. Intuit handles the sign-in. The software never sees a QuickBooks password.

Reconnecting

If the connection expires or is disconnected, the same authorization step is run again. Reconnecting restores access to accounting data only; it recovers nothing that was deleted and changes no records.
