RR Financials

Internal back office software for Ruoff Restaurants

The QuickBooks connection has ended

RR Financials no longer has access to that QuickBooks Online company. Its authorization was revoked and it cannot read or write anything there until someone authorizes it again.

What this changed in QuickBooks

Nothing. Disconnecting removes the software's access. Journal entries it posted while it was connected stay in the books exactly as they are, the same as any entry made by a person, and can be reviewed or reversed in QuickBooks in the usual way.

Accounting records already held

Accounting data read before the disconnection remains in the company's own records for as long as its retention requirements demand, and is then deleted. To ask about that data, write to ruoff.restaurants@gmail.com.

To reconnect

An authorized person runs the software's authorization step again from a company computer. See connecting and reconnecting.
