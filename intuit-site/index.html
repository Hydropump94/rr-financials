RR Financials

Internal back office software for Ruoff Restaurants

Private software, used by one company on its own books

RR Financials is software Ruoff Restaurants built and runs for its own accounting and restaurant operations. It is not sold, licensed or offered to anyone else, and there is no public sign-up.

What it does

It reads the company's own accounting, payroll, point of sale and delivery data, checks the month's close against the source records, and proposes the journal entries that close the month. A named person approves each entry before anything is written back to QuickBooks Online. Nothing posts on its own.

Its QuickBooks connection

The software connects to Ruoff Restaurants' own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's authorized OAuth 2.0 flow. It holds no credentials for any other company and asks for no access beyond the accounting data of the company that authorized it.

This site exists because Intuit's app settings require a published license agreement, privacy policy and contact page for any application that connects to QuickBooks Online. It is not a product page.
