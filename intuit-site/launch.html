RR Financials

Internal back office software for Ruoff Restaurants

RR Financials runs on the company's own computers

There is no web application to open here. RR Financials is internal software operated by Ruoff Restaurants staff on company machines, not a hosted service that users sign in to.

If you reached this page from QuickBooks Online

You clicked through from the app's entry in your QuickBooks apps list. Nothing further is needed on this page, and nothing on this page can change your accounting data.

If you need to reach someone

Write to ruoff.restaurants@gmail.com and say which QuickBooks company you are asking about.

To end the software's access to a QuickBooks company at any time, open QuickBooks Online, go to Apps, find RR Financials, and disconnect it. Access stops immediately.
