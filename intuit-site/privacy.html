RR Financials

Internal back office software for Ruoff Restaurants

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

RR Financials is private software used by Ruoff Restaurants for its own accounting and operations. It is not offered to the public and it has no other users.

What it accesses

With the company's authorization through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 consent, the software reads the company's own QuickBooks Online accounting data: the chart of accounts, locations, classes, journal entries and financial reports. It writes journal entries only where a named person has approved that specific entry. It requests no access to any other QuickBooks company.

What it stores, and where

Accounting data is stored in a database on company-controlled computers. Authorization tokens are stored in a local configuration file that is not shared and is not committed to source control. No accounting data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Personal information

The software handles employee payroll totals and vendor records that come from the company's own systems, for accounting purposes only. It does not collect information from the public, and this site has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third party scripts.

How long it is kept

Accounting records are kept as long as the company's record retention requirements demand, and are then deleted.

Disconnecting

The connection to QuickBooks can be revoked at any time from within QuickBooks Online or by the company, which immediately ends the software's access.
